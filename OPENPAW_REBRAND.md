## 📖 ABOUT SECTION

### **What is OpenPaw?**

OpenPaw makes it easy to run your own AI agent in the cloud. Think of it as your personal AI assistant—available 24/7, accessible via messaging apps, and smart enough to help with everything from answering questions to automating your workflows.

We believe everyone should have access to powerful AI without needing a DevOps degree. That's why we handle all the infrastructure, scaling, and technical complexity. You just chat with your agent and get things done.

### **Why We Built OpenPaw**

We're developers who love OpenClaw—an amazing open-source AI agent platform. But setting it up on AWS? That was hours of pain: Docker configs, ECS tasks, Bedrock permissions... We knew there had to be an easier way.

So we built OpenPaw: all the power of OpenClaw, none of the infrastructure headaches. Now anyone can deploy their own AI agent in minutes, not hours.

### **Who's It For?**

- **Individuals** who want a personal AI assistant
- **Developers** building side projects without the ops burden
- **Small teams** automating workflows together
- **Businesses** looking for a private, customizable AI solution

Whether you're a curious beginner or a seasoned pro, OpenPaw welcomes you. 🐾

### **Pricing FAQ Section**

**Q: What counts as a "message"?**  
A: Each conversation turn (your prompt + AI response) is one message. Typical cost: about $0.009/message.

**Q: Do my credits expire?**  
A: Nope! Your credits stay in your account until you use them.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**

**Q: What happens if I run out of credits?**

## 📝 FINAL NOTES

**Tone Dos:**
- ✅ Use "we" and "you" (personal, direct)
- ✅ Ask questions (engage the reader)
- ✅ Use simple language (avoid jargon)
- ✅ Be encouraging ("You can do this!")
- ✅ Show personality (emoji where appropriate 🐾)

**Tone Don'ts:**
- ❌ Avoid "utilize" (just say "use")
- ❌ Don't oversell ("revolutionary," "game-changing" sparingly)
- ❌ Don't be dismissive of competitors
- ❌ Don't use ALL CAPS for emphasis
- ❌ Don't be too casual (still professional)

**Call to Action:**
Every page should have a clear next step. Whether it's "Sign up," "Learn more," or "Contact us," make it obvious what the user should do next.
