## 🎨 Design Principles

1. **Bold Headlines:** Short, punchy, benefit-driven
2. **GIFs/Videos:** Show don't tell (browser automation, multi-channel)

4. **Social Proof:** Numbers, use cases, testimonials
5. **Simplicity:** Remove jargon, focus on outcomes
6. **Mobile-First:** Most users browse on phones
