### Competitor 1: SimpleClaw.com
**Positioning:** "Deploy OpenClaw under 1 minute"  
**Target:** Non-technical users who want OpenClaw  
**Pricing:** Unknown (not shown on landing page)  
**Value Prop:** Speed + simplicity ("avoid all technical complexity")  
**Differentiation:** Pre-provisioned servers, one-click deploy  
**Messaging:** Functional, technical, developer-focused

### Competitor 2: MyClaw.ai
**Positioning:** "Your OpenClaw ready for you"  
**Target:** Power users, developers, professionals  
**Pricing:** $19/mo (Lite), $39/mo (Pro), $79/mo (Max)  
**Value Prop:** "Personal AI assistant everyone's obsessed with"  
**Differentiation:** "Always online, always ready" + "No setup needed"  
**Messaging:** Aspirational, productivity-focused, slightly warmer

### What They Have in Common:
- Both are "managed OpenClaw hosting"
- Both target technical friction (setup complexity)
- Both emphasize privacy ("your own instance")
- Both are **already in market** with paying customers
