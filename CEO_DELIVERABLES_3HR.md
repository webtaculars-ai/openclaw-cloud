## ✅ DELIVERABLE 5: CUSTOMER INTERVIEW QUESTIONS (30 MIN)

### Goal: Validate "Helping Paw" Positioning

**Target:** 10 interviews before launch

**Screening Questions:**
1. Do you currently use AI tools? (Yes = qualified)
2. How often? (Looking for regular users)
3. Would you pay for AI? (Must be willing)

**Interview Script (20 minutes):**

**Part 1: Current Behavior (5 min)**
1. What AI tools do you use today?
2. How do they make you FEEL? (emotion, not features)
3. What frustrates you about current AI?
4. What do you love?
5. If you could change one thing, what would it be?

**Part 2: Concept Test (10 min)**
6. [Show landing page] First impressions?
7. "Like having a smart friend in your pocket" - react to this
8. Does "helping paw" resonate? Why/why not?
9. What would make you try this?
10. What would make you PAY for this?

**Part 3: Pricing (3 min)**
11. Current AI spend: $___/month
12. React to our pricing ($5, $15, $50)
13. Which tier would you choose? Why?
14. Pay-as-you-go vs subscription: which do you prefer?

**Part 4: Objections (2 min)**
15. What concerns do you have?
16. What questions are unanswered?
17. What would stop you from trying?

**Wrap-Up:**
18. On a scale of 1-10, how likely to try? (9-10 = very good)
19. Can I send you early access?
20. Know anyone else who might be interested?

### What We're Validating
- ✓ "Helping paw" messaging resonates
- ✓ Pricing is perceived as fair
- ✓ Emotional positioning (friend vs tool) works
- ✓ Primary objections are addressable
- ✓ Target market is correctly identified

### Red Flags to Watch
- ❌ "I don't get it"
- ❌ "This is the same as ChatGPT"
- ❌ "Too expensive for what it is"
- ❌ "I don't trust Telegram"
- ❌ "Warmth doesn't matter to me"

If 3+ people say same red flag → pivot messaging
