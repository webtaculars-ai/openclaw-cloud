### Who should use OpenPaw?

- Anyone wanting a "friend" not a "tool"

## Getting Started

### How do I sign up?
1. Go to openpaw.co
2. Click "Meet Your New Friend"
3. Create an account (email + password)
4. Add credits or use a promo code

### How do I connect Telegram?
After signing up, go to your dashboard and follow the setup guide. You'll create a Telegram bot and connect it to OpenPaw. Takes about 5 minutes.

### Do I need technical skills?
No! If you can send a text message, you can use OpenPaw. It's designed for everyone, not just tech experts.

## Privacy & Security

### Is my data private?
Yes. Your conversations are encrypted and stored securely on AWS. We never sell your data or share it with third parties (except our AI provider, Anthropic, which processes your messages to generate responses).

### Who can see my messages?
Only you and the AI. OpenPaw staff do not read your conversations unless you explicitly request support.

### Is it GDPR compliant?
Yes. You have the right to access, export, or delete your data at any time. Email privacy@openpaw.co to exercise these rights.

### Where is my data stored?
Your data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) servers with encryption at rest and in transit.

## Troubleshooting

### I'm not receiving responses
- Check your credit balance
- Verify your Telegram bot is connected
- Check if your message went through
- Contact support@openpaw.co if issues persist

### My promo code isn't working
- Make sure you've typed it correctly (case-insensitive)
- Check if it's already been used
- Verify it hasn't expired
- Contact support if you believe it should work

### I forgot my password
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. We'll send you a reset email.

### How do I delete my account?
Email support@openpaw.co and we'll delete your account within 30 days.
