### **Subscription Plans**

| Plan | Price | Credits/Month | Best For |
|------|-------|---------------|----------|

### **Credit System**

- All plans operate on a **prepaid credit system**

  - Browser automation tasks
- **Credits never expire** and roll over monthly
- Users can purchase additional credit packs without subscribing

### **Pricing Model**
- Pay-as-you-go: Only charged for active AI usage
- Auto-stop feature: Agents hibernate when idle (no credit consumption)
- Transparent dashboard showing per-interaction costs
- No hidden fees
