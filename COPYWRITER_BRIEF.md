## ✍️ WRITING GUIDELINES

### Do:
- ✅ Use "you" (direct address)
- ✅ Short sentences (8-15 words avg)
- ✅ Active voice ("Save money" not "Money is saved")
- ✅ Specific numbers ($9, 2,000 messages)
- ✅ Benefits over features ("Never run out" not "Auto-reload")
- ✅ Show, don't tell ("$9 vs $19" not "affordable")

### Don't:
- ❌ Jargon (ECS, Fargate, vCPU)
- ❌ Passive voice
- ❌ Weasel words ("might", "could", "perhaps")
- ❌ Clichés ("game-changer", "revolutionary")
- ❌ All caps or excessive punctuation!!!
