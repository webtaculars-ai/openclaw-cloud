At OpenPaw, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our managed AI agent platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an OpenPaw account, we collect:

  • Email address - For account identification and communication
  • Password - Stored encrypted for account security
  • Name (optional) - For personalization

1.2 Agent Configuration Data

To provide our service, we collect:

  • Messaging app tokens (Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord) - Stored encrypted to connect your AI agent
  • Agent settings - Your preferences and configurations
  • Agent name and description - For identification in your dashboard

1.3 Usage Data

We automatically collect:

  • AI interactions - Number of messages, tokens consumed, features used
  • Credit transactions - Purchase history, credit balance, consumption patterns
  • System logs - Error messages, agent status, performance metrics
  • Technical information - IP address, browser type, device information

1.4 Payment Information

Payment processing is handled by Lemon Squeezy (our payment processor). We do not store your credit card information. We receive:

  • Transaction confirmations - Amount paid, date, payment method type (not full card details)
  • Billing address (if provided to payment processor)

1.5 Conversation Data

Important: We do not store the content of your conversations with your AI agent. Your messages are:

  • Processed through AWS Bedrock (Anthropic's Claude AI)
  • Not retained by OpenPaw after processing
  • Not used to train AI models
  • Not accessible to OpenPaw staff unless you explicitly request support and share logs

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Service Delivery

  • Provision and manage your AI agents
  • Process AI requests through AWS Bedrock
  • Track credit usage and billing
  • Monitor system performance and uptime

2.2 Communication

  • Send account-related emails (confirmations, receipts, alerts)
  • Notify you of service updates or outages
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send marketing emails (only with your consent, unsubscribe anytime)

2.3 Improvement and Analytics

  • Analyze aggregated, anonymized usage patterns
  • Improve service performance and features
  • Detect and prevent abuse or fraud
  • Troubleshoot technical issues

2.4 Legal Compliance

  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Respond to legal requests (subpoenas, court orders)
  • Protect our rights and property
  • Prevent fraud or abuse

3. How We Protect Your Data

3.1 Encryption

  • In transit: All data transmitted between you and OpenPaw is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • At rest: Data stored in AWS is encrypted using AWS-managed encryption
  • Passwords: Hashed using industry-standard bcrypt
  • API tokens: Encrypted in our database

3.2 Access Controls

  • Limited OpenPaw staff access (only when necessary for support)
  • Role-based access controls (RBAC)
  • Multi-factor authentication for admin access
  • Regular security audits

3.3 Infrastructure Security

  • Hosted on AWS (SOC 2, ISO 27001 certified)
  • Regular security patches and updates
  • Isolated container environments per user
  • Automated backups (encrypted)

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 AWS Bedrock (AI Processing)

  • Purpose: Process AI requests through Anthropic's Claude models
  • Data shared: Your prompts and AI responses (not stored by us)
  • Privacy: AWS does not train models on your data
  • Policy: AWS Privacy Notice

4.2 Lemon Squeezy (Payment Processing)

  • Purpose: Process subscription payments and credit purchases
  • Data shared: Email, payment amount, transaction details
  • Security: PCI-DSS compliant
  • Policy: Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy

4.3 Messaging Platforms

  • Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Signal: Used to deliver AI responses
  • Data shared: Messages you send/receive through our agent
  • Note: These platforms have their own privacy policies

5. Your Privacy Rights

5.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive your data in machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to processing for certain purposes
  • Withdraw consent: Opt out of marketing emails anytime

5.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you are in California, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Know if we sell or share your personal information (we don't)
  • Access your personal information
  • Delete your personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

5.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To request access, deletion, or correction of your data, email us at: privacy@openpaw.co

We will respond within 30 days and verify your identity before processing requests.

6. Data Retention

6.1 Active Accounts

  • Your account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Usage logs retained for 90 days (for billing and support)
  • Transaction history retained indefinitely (for accounting and legal compliance)

6.2 Deleted Accounts

  • Account data deleted within 30 days of account closure
  • Backup data may persist for up to 90 days
  • Transaction records retained for 7 years (tax/legal requirements)

6.3 Conversation Data

  • We do not retain conversation content
  • Only usage metadata (tokens consumed, timestamp) is stored

7. Cookies and Tracking

7.1 Essential Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in (session cookies)
  • Remember your preferences
  • Provide basic functionality

7.2 Analytics (Optional)

We may use analytics tools to understand how users interact with our service. You can opt out via browser settings or "Do Not Track" signals.

7.3 No Third-Party Advertising

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or sell your data to advertisers.

8. Children's Privacy

OpenPaw is not intended for users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover a child's account, we will delete it immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

OpenPaw is hosted on AWS infrastructure, which may involve data transfer across borders. We ensure:

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • AWS complies with GDPR and international data protection standards
  • Standard contractual clauses (SCCs) are in place where required

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will:

  • Be posted at https://www.openpaw.co/privacy
  • Take effect immediately upon posting
  • Be communicated via email for significant changes

Continued use of the service after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your privacy rights:

  • Email: privacy@openpaw.co or support@openpaw.co
  • Website: https://www.openpaw.co
  • Response Time: Within 30 days

12. Your Consent

By using OpenPaw, you consent to this Privacy Policy and our collection, use, and protection of your data as described here.
