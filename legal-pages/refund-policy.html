Refund Policy

Last Updated: February 20, 2026

At OpenPaw, we strive for complete customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy explains when refunds are available and how to request them.

1. Overview

1.1 Credit-Based System

OpenPaw operates on a prepaid credit system. Credits are purchased in advance and consumed based on actual usage of AI services, browser automation, and infrastructure resources.

1.2 General Policy

Credits are generally non-refundable once purchased, except as outlined in this policy or as required by law.

2. Refund Eligibility

2.1 Full Refund Scenarios

You are eligible for a 100% refund in the following cases:

A. Service Failure (Within 14 Days)

If your agent fails to provision successfully due to issues on our end:

  • Agent provisioning fails repeatedly
  • Technical errors prevent agent from starting
  • Critical features completely non-functional
  • Infrastructure issues prevent service access

Requirements:

  • Must be reported within 14 days of purchase
  • Must provide error details or screenshots
  • Must cooperate with troubleshooting attempts

B. Unused Credits (Within 14 Days)

If you have not used any credits and request cancellation:

  • Credits have not been consumed
  • No agents have been provisioned
  • Request made within 14 days of purchase

C. Billing Errors

If we charged your account in error:

  • Duplicate charges
  • Incorrect amount charged
  • Charges after account closure
  • System errors causing overcharges

No time limit - report billing errors as soon as discovered.

2.2 Partial Refund Scenarios

You may be eligible for a partial refund in these cases:

A. Technical Issues (Within 14 Days)

If service issues significantly impact your experience:

  • Frequent downtime or service interruptions
  • Major features not working as advertised
  • Performance significantly below expectations

Refund Amount:

  • Remaining unused credits only
  • Credits consumed for working services are not refunded
  • Pro-rated based on actual usage

B. Service Dissatisfaction (Within 7 Days)

If you're unsatisfied with the service:

  • Service doesn't meet your needs
  • Feature limitations not clearly communicated
  • Changed your mind about using the service

Refund Amount:

  • Remaining unused credits only
  • Subject to 10% administrative fee
  • Must be requested within 7 days

2.3 No Refund Scenarios

Refunds are NOT available in these cases:

A. Normal Usage

  • Credits consumed for successful AI interactions
  • Browser automation tasks that completed
  • Agent infrastructure that functioned as intended
  • After 14-day refund window

B. User Error

  • Incorrect configuration by user
  • User did not follow setup instructions
  • User violated Terms of Service
  • User chose wrong pricing tier

C. Third-Party Issues

  • Telegram/WhatsApp/Discord platform issues
  • AWS Bedrock service interruptions (beyond our control)
  • Internet connectivity problems
  • Device compatibility issues

D. Account Violations

  • Account terminated for abuse
  • Terms of Service violations
  • Fraudulent activity
  • Chargebacks filed

3. Refund Process

3.1 How to Request a Refund

Step 1: Contact Support

Email: support@openpaw.co

Include:

  • Your account email
  • Purchase date and amount
  • Reason for refund request
  • Detailed description of issues (if applicable)
  • Screenshots or error messages (if applicable)

Step 2: Verification

We will:

  • Verify your account and purchase
  • Review your usage history
  • Investigate technical issues (if claimed)
  • Determine eligibility based on this policy

Step 3: Decision

You will receive a response within 48 business hours with:

  • Approval or denial of refund
  • Refund amount (if approved)
  • Explanation of decision
  • Next steps

Step 4: Processing

If approved:

  • Refund processed within 5-7 business days
  • Returned to original payment method
  • Confirmation email sent
  • Account access may be revoked

3.2 Appeal Process

If your refund request is denied:

  1. Reply to the decision email with additional information
  2. We will review within 48 hours
  3. Final decision provided
  4. You may escalate to payment provider if you disagree

4. Special Circumstances

4.1 Service Outages

Minor Outages (< 4 hours):

  • No refund
  • Service credit may be offered

Major Outages (> 4 hours in 24-hour period):

  • Pro-rated credit for downtime
  • Applied to account automatically

Extended Outages (> 24 hours):

  • Pro-rated refund available upon request
  • Or equivalent credit bonus

4.2 Feature Changes

If we:

  • Remove major features
  • Significantly change service functionality
  • Increase prices substantially

You may request a refund of unused credits within 30 days of the change.

4.3 Account Termination by Us

If we terminate your account for reasons other than Terms of Service violations:

  • Unused credits will be refunded
  • Pro-rated for partial month if applicable

5. Credit Expiration

5.1 No Expiration

Credits purchased do not expire. However:

  • Inactive accounts (>12 months) may be closed
  • Unused credits in closed accounts are forfeited
  • We will send warnings before closing inactive accounts

5.2 Account Closure

If you close your account:

  • Unused credits are forfeited (no refund after 14 days)
  • Request refund before closing account
  • Account data will be deleted per our retention policy

6. Chargebacks

6.1 Contact Us First

Before filing a chargeback, please contact us. Chargebacks:

  • Result in immediate account suspension
  • Forfeit all credits
  • May result in permanent ban
  • Incur administrative fees

6.2 Disputing Chargebacks

If you file a chargeback:

  • We will provide evidence to payment processor
  • Your account will remain suspended pending resolution
  • If chargeback is reversed, account may be restored
  • If chargeback succeeds, account remains closed

7. Exceptions and Discretion

7.1 Extraordinary Circumstances

We reserve the right to issue refunds outside this policy for:

  • Exceptional hardship cases
  • Customer service goodwill
  • Our errors or mistakes
  • Legal requirements

7.2 Final Authority

All refund decisions are at OpenPaw's sole discretion and are final, except as required by law or payment processor policies.

8. Modifications to This Policy

8.1 Changes

We may update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will:

  • Be posted at https://www.openpaw.co/refund-policy
  • Take effect immediately upon posting
  • Apply to purchases made after the change

8.2 Notification

We will make reasonable efforts to notify users of significant policy changes via:

  • Email to registered address
  • Notice on website
  • In-app notification

9. Legal Rights

9.1 Consumer Protection

This policy does not affect your statutory rights under applicable consumer protection laws.

9.2 Jurisdiction-Specific Rights

Depending on your location, you may have additional refund rights under local law. Nothing in this policy waives those rights.

10. Contact Information

For refund requests or questions about this policy:

  • Email: support@openpaw.co
  • Website: https://www.openpaw.co
  • Response Time: Within 48 business hours
